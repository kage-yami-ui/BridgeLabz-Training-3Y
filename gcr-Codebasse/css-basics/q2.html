City Overview

Kyoto was Japan's capital and emperor's residence for over a thousand years. It seamlessly bridges ancient historic traditions with modern urban vitality.

Historic Attractions

Famous for thousands of classical Buddhist temples, Shinto shrines, historic gardens, and traditional wooden machiya houses.

Rich Culture & Heritage

From formal multi-course kaiseki dining to artisan crafts and tea ceremonies, Kyoto represents the peak of classical Japanese aesthetics.

Plan Your Visit

Spring cherry blossoms and vibrant autumn foliage make Kyoto an unmissable destination throughout the year.
