Top 3 Travel Destinations

Paris, France

Known as the City of Light, Paris is famous for its art, gastronomy, and historic landmarks.

"Paris is always a good idea."

Kyoto, Japan

Famous for its classical Buddhist temples, gardens, imperial palaces, and wooden houses.

"A serene blend of history and breathtaking nature."

Rome, Italy

A sprawling cosmopolitan city with nearly 3,000 years of globally influential art and architecture.

"Walking through Rome is like walking through living history."

Travel Tips

  • Keep physical copies of travel documents.
  • Pack light and comfortable shoes.
  • Learn basic local phrases.
